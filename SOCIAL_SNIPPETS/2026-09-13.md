# Social snippets — 2026-09-13 (Run #3)

## LinkedIn
Today's Sporewick session was all plumbing, and it was overdue: the
game had no save file. Close the app mid-grow and you lost everything —
not exactly "cozy idle game" behavior. Fixed it by autosaving on every
action (plant, harvest, combine) instead of relying on a clean-exit
hook, since mobile OSes kill backgrounded apps without warning. The
harder part was offline catch-up: if a plot would've finished growing
while the app was closed, it needs to be ready the moment you reopen,
not stuck mid-timer. Tested that specifically by planting a spore,
manually back-dating the save file's timestamp past a full grow cycle,
and reloading — the plot came back "ready to harvest," which is exactly
what a player closing the app for an hour should see. 17 days left in
the submission window.
